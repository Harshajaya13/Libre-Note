<h1 align="center">Libre Notes</h1>
<h3 align="center">A clean, local-first, and completely unrestricted fork of Notesnook.</h3>

## Why was Libre Notes created?

You might be wondering: *"Why does this exist when Notesnook already has their own site and app?"*

We wanted a completely **local-first** experience. Because we are storing everything locally on our own devices and don't need to rely on their cloud servers, it didn't make sense to be held back by paywalls and artificial restrictions. 

No one should be forced to adjust and limit themselves to only 10 notes just to write down their thoughts. We believe that if you are using your own device's storage, you should have infinite freedom to write as much as you want. That is exactly why we came together to create Libre Notes: to give you a sanctuary for your thoughts with zero boundaries, zero subscriptions, and 100% control over your own data.
